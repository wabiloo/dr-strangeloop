# Player logos (small icons on the in-browser test page)

- dashjs.png: `samples/lib/img/dashjs-logo.png` in https://github.com/Dash-Industry-Forum/dash.js
- hlsjs.svg: `docs/logo.svg` in https://github.com/video-dev/hls.js
- shaka.png: https://shaka-project.github.io/logo.png (mark only, cropped, converted to white on transparent)
- videojs.png: `site/public/logo-white.png` in https://github.com/videojs/video.js
- bitmovin.png: https://searchvectorlogo.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/bitmovin-inc-logo-vector.png (opaque white background removed, cropped, converted to white on transparent)

All are shown as flat white silhouettes on the page's dark background (CSS `filter: brightness(0) invert(1)`), so any transparent-background logo works.
